CUMBERLAND, Md. — March 19, 2026 — Fort Hill High School proudly celebrated academic excellence, leadership and service during an Honor Society Induction Ceremony held on Tuesday, March 10. The ceremony recognized students from four organizations: Sociedad Honoraria Hispánica (Spanish Honor Society), the National Art Honor Society, the Rho Kappa National Social Studies Honor Society and the Science National Honor Society. It honored the achievements of 68 newly inducted members and recognized 48 graduating seniors.

Membership in these honor societies represents more than academic success. Students selected for induction have distinguished themselves through a combination of strong academic performance, leadership within the school community, a commitment to service, and a passion for their respective fields of study.
